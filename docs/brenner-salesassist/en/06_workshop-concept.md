# 6. Workshop concept: "Following up with AI" (90 minutes)

**Audience:** Brenner sales team (8–15 people, no AI experience) · **Format:** on site, everyone with their own laptop and Langdock access
**Goal:** By the end, everyone has created **at least two real follow-up emails** with the assistant and committed to using it over the next four weeks.
**Principle:** 70% doing, 30% listening. People work on their own real open quotes (anonymised), not on slides.

| Time | Block | Content and method | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | **Arrival and starting point** | Ms. Brandt opens (2 min, showing management backing). Quick show of hands: "How many follow-up emails per week? How long does one take?" The result goes on the flipchart as the **baseline**. | Shared view of the problem, baseline values |
| 10–20 | **AI in 10 minutes** | What AI can and can't do, using one example: good wording yes, facts no. Plus the rule of thumb "You send it, not the AI". Concerns are addressed openly ("Will this replace me?" → No, it takes over the typing). | Realistic expectations |
| 20–35 | **Live demo** | Trainer shows the three cases (standard, incomplete, sensitive) and lets the group guess first: "What do you think it does with the discount request?" | Trust in the guardrails |
| 35–60 | **Hands-on, round 1** | Pairs: everyone takes **one of their own open quotes** (anonymised) and creates the email. The partner checks it with a checklist card (Facts correct? One next step? Would I send this?). Trainer walks around and helps. | 1st real email per person |
| 60–70 | **Review** | 3–4 examples shown on the projector: What was good? Where did it need rework? Feedback on the assistant is collected live on an "improvement list". | Learning, input for prompt v1.1 |
| 70–82 | **Hands-on, round 2** | Advanced features: "shorter", "2nd reminder", a sensitive case from their own work. Faster participants help slower ones. | 2nd email, more confidence |
| 82–90 | **Commitment and close** | Everyone writes their **personal goal** on a card (e.g. "all follow-up emails in the next 4 weeks with SalesAssist"). Two **AI champions** from the team are named. Short feedback (scale 1–5 and one sentence). Outlook: check-in in 2 weeks, evaluation after 4 weeks. | Commitment, go-to people |

**Materials:** link to the assistant, cheat sheet (one A5 page: input template, 3 rules, commands like "shorter"), checklist cards, flipchart, the video for reference.
**Success factors:** management visibly takes part · real cases instead of exercises · a success within the first 35 minutes · champions for the time afterwards.

**How I'll know after 4 weeks whether the workshop worked (details in `07_success-measurement.md`):**
1. **Usage:** ≥ 70% of the team use the assistant at least once a week (Langdock analytics).
2. **Time:** The self-reported time per follow-up email drops from the baseline (e.g. ~12 min) to ≤ 5 min.
3. **Quality:** In a sample, ≥ 80% of emails meet the checklist (facts correct, one next step, no stock phrases).
4. **Behaviour:** More open quotes are followed up within 10 days than before.
