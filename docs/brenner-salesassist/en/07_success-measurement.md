# 7. Success measurement – KPIs and evaluation after four weeks

**Key question:** Does SalesAssist save the sales team time, reliably improve customer communication, and actually get used?
**Principle:** Few metrics, collected with little effort, with a baseline taken **before** the workshop. All targets are **proposals**
and will be agreed with Ms. Brandt at kick-off.

## KPI set

| Dimension | KPI | Method | Baseline (week 0) | Target after 4 weeks (proposal) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Usage** | Active users per week (share of team) | Langdock usage statistics for the assistant | 0% (new assistant) | ≥ 70% |
| | Follow-up emails created per week | Langdock conversations with the assistant | 0 | ≥ 60% of all follow-up emails in the team |
| **Time** | Avg. minutes per follow-up email | Short survey (self-estimate) plus stopwatch sample with 3 people | Workshop show of hands | ≤ 5 min (or −50%) |
| | Projected time saved per month | (baseline − new) × number of emails | – | report as a result |
| **Quality** | Share of emails meeting the checklist | Sample of 10 sent emails per week, checked by sales management or an AI champion using the checklist from `03` | Sample of 10 old emails | ≥ 80% |
| | Critical errors (invented facts, unapproved discount) | Sample plus reports from the team | – | **0** |
| | Rework rate | Short survey: "How much did you have to change the draft?" (barely / some / a lot) | – | ≥ 70% "barely" or "some" |
| **Impact** | Follow-up rate: open quotes followed up within 10 days | CRM/ERP report or quote list | Previous month | +20 percentage points |
| | Reply rate to follow-up emails | CRM or self-reporting | Previous month | Watch the trend (leading indicator, no target) |
| **Acceptance** | Satisfaction and recommendation | Survey after 4 weeks (scale 1–5 plus free text) | Workshop feedback | Avg. ≥ 4.0 |

> **Note on interpretation:** Win rate is **not** a meaningful KPI after 4 weeks, because decision cycles in plumbing/heating project business
> are often longer and many other factors play a role. It is only looked at after 3–6 months.

## Timeline

| When | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Week 0 (before the workshop) | Collect baseline: show of hands, sample of 10 old emails, follow-up rate of the previous month | Consultant and Ms. Brandt |
| Week 0 | Workshop (90 min), prompt v1.0 live | Consultant |
| Week 1 | Prompt v1.1 from the workshop improvement list | Consultant |
| Week 2 | Check-in (15 min, in the team meeting): stumbling blocks, tips from the AI champions, usage status | AI champions |
| Weekly | Quality sample (10 emails, about 20 min) | Sales management or AI champion |
| Week 4 | Final survey (5 questions, 3 min), 3 short interviews (10 min each), analysis | Consultant |
| Week 4 | Results report (1 page) and decision meeting with Ms. Brandt | Consultant |

## Evaluation after four weeks

**Final survey (5 questions):**
1. How often did you use SalesAssist in the last 2 weeks? (never / 1–2× / 3–5× / more)
2. How long does a follow-up email take you now? (minutes)
3. How much did you have to change the drafts on average? (barely / some / a lot)
4. How satisfied are you overall? (1–5)
5. What stops you from using it more often, or what should be better? (free text)

**Short interviews (3 people: one heavy user, one occasional user, one person who doesn't use it):**
Which situation was especially helpful? Where did it not fit? What would need to happen for you to use it every time?

**Decision rules:**

| Result | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Usage ≥ 70%, quality ≥ 80%, 0 critical errors | **Scale:** second assistant "meeting notes after a customer visit", knowledge files (stage 2), assess CRM integration |
| Usage 40–70% **or** quality 60–80% | **Refine:** address causes from the interviews (prompt, access, habit), 30-min refresher, measure again after 4 weeks |
| Usage < 40% **or** critical errors occurred | **Reorient:** clarify barriers (access, trust, wrong use case), fix the causes of errors, restart with a smaller pilot group if needed |

**Results report (1 page):** KPI table with baseline, actual and target · projected time saved in hours per month · three quotes from the team · prompt changes v1.0 → v1.x · recommendation with next step.
